# GridPulse: Smart Energy Demand Forecasting and Optimization
Predict electricity demand for the next hour or day, detect unusual consumption, and recommend when
demand could be shifted to reduce peak loads.

Free data: U.S. Energy Information Administration API provides electricity demand and other energy
series; weather data can be added from Open-Meteo.

Models: XGBoost, Random Forest, LightGBM, or a seasonal baseline.

Why it is valuable: Forecasting, time-based validation, changing seasonal patterns, cost-aware
decisions, and measurable prediction errors.
